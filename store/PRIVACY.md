# Privacy Policy — Anything To Markdown

**Last updated:** 12 September 2026

Anything To Markdown is a desktop application that converts documents on your own
computer into Markdown files.

## The short version

The app collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing about you.

## What the app does with your files

When you convert a document, the app reads that file from your disk, converts it
in memory on your computer, and writes a `.md` file to the location you chose.
That is the whole lifecycle.

Your documents are never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with the
developers or any third party. The application has no network features and works
with no internet connection at all.

## Data we collect

None. Specifically, Anything To Markdown has:

- no user accounts, sign-in or registration
- no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking
- no advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- no cookies or web content
- no background or automatic updates of its own (updates arrive through the
  Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's own privacy terms)

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to store, share, sell or
disclose, and no data to request access to or deletion of.

## Data the app stores on your device

Only the Markdown files you ask it to produce, at the location you choose. These
are ordinary files that belong to you; uninstalling the app does not delete them.

The app is installed as an MSIX package, so Windows keeps its application data in
a private per-user folder. Uninstalling removes that folder along with the app.

## Optional components

Two file types rely on separate tools that you install yourself, if you want
them:

- **FFmpeg**, used when transcribing audio files
- **ExifTool**, used when reading image metadata

These run locally on your machine as well. They are not bundled with the app and
are governed by their own licences.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.
